# Research and citation

SafeDrones implements and extends research on reliability evaluation for
multi-rotor propulsion, batteries, processors, GPS availability, and real-time
dependability identities.

## Primary publications

### Propulsion-system reliability

K. Aslansefat, F. Marques, R. Mendonça, and J. Barata, “A Markov Process-Based
Approach for Reliability Evaluation of the Propulsion System in Multi-rotor
Drones,” *Doctoral Conference on Computing, Electrical and Industrial Systems*,
2019, pp. 91–98.

- [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-17771-3_8)
- [PDF in this repository](https://github.com/Dependable-Intelligent-Systems-Lab/SafeDrones/blob/master/Documents/A%20Markov%20Process-Based%20Approach%20for%20Reliability%20Evaluation%20of%20the%20Propulsion%20System%20in%20Multi-rotor%20Drones.pdf)

### Real-time reliability evaluation

K. Aslansefat, P. Nikolaou, M. Walker, M. N. Akram, I. Sorokos, J. Reich,
*et al.*, “SafeDrones: Real-Time Reliability Evaluation of UAVs Using Executable
Digital Dependable Identities,” *Model-Based Safety and Assessment (IMBSA)*,
2022, pp. 252–266.

- [Presentation](https://github.com/Dependable-Intelligent-Systems-Lab/SafeDrones/blob/master/Documents/SafeDrones_Real-Time%20Reliability%20Evaluation%20of%20UAVs%20using%20Executable%20Digital%20Dependable%20Identities_IMBSA2022.pptx)
- [Paper PDF](https://github.com/Dependable-Intelligent-Systems-Lab/SafeDrones/blob/master/Documents/SafeDrones_Real-Time%20Reliability%20Evaluation%20of%20UAVs%20using%20Executable%20Digital%20Dependable%20Identities_IMBSA2022.pdf)
